### 🌾 AgroReef — Agentic AI for Agriculture  
`Agentic AI` `React` `TypeScript` `Supabase` `Gemini` `PWA`

An agentic AI agricultural advisory platform designed for Pakistani farmers. AgroReef combines weather intelligence, crop-disease analysis, market awareness, voice interaction, multilingual support, field monitoring, alerts, and human-centered decision support.

**Core capabilities**

- Seven-agent architecture with Supervisor, Vision, Weather, Market, Critic, Reasoning, and Executive agents
- OPARD loop: Observe → Plan → Act → Reflect → Deliver
- Urdu and English voice interaction
- Crop disease detection through image analysis
- GPS-based weather and seven-day forecast
- Pakistani mandi market prices and sell/hold recommendations
- Field mapping, notebooks, alerts, and farmer-specific records
- Progressive Web App with secure Supabase authentication and Row-Level Security

🔗 [Repository](https://github.com/AshubShafqat/AgroReef-FYP) · [Live Demo](https://agroreef.lovable.app/)
